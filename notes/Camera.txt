
## 2D Camera System

1. The Transformation Logic
The core of every camera system is the conversion formula. To find where an object should appear on your screen:

ScreenX = (WorldX - CamX) * Zoom
ScreenY = (WorldY - CamY) * zoom

Panning - Increases or decreases CamX and CamY
Zooming - Increases or decreases Zoom multiplier

2. Implementation System

	i) 
		Method - Manual Scalling
		
		You calculate the SDL_Rect for every object manually before calling SDL_RenderCopy.
	
	ii)
		Method - SDL_RenderSetScale
		
		Uses the hardware to scale everything drawn after the call.
