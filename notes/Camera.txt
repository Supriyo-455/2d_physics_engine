
## 2D Camera System

1. The Transformation Logic
The core of every camera system is the conversion formula. To find where an object should appear on your screen:

As camera position is at center, we need to calculate camera position relative to upper left corner...

OffsetX = ScreenX - (Game->Camera.Width / 2.0f);
OffsetY = ScreenY - (Game->Camera.Height / 2.0f);
            
WorldX = Game->Camera.Position.x + (OffsetX / Game->Camera.Zoom);
WorldY = Game->Camera.Position.y + (OffsetY / Game->Camera.Zoom);

Panning - Increases or decreases CamX and CamY
Zooming - Increases or decreases Zoom multiplier
