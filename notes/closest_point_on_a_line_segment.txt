How to find closest point on a line segment


Consider, line A to B; and outside random point P

A *-----------------* B


			*P


Basic Algorithm

V(x) -> Vector x
. -> Dot product
|| -> Modulus
^x -> to the power x

1. V(AB) = V(B) - V(A)
2. V(AP) = V(P) - V(A)
3. Proj = V(AB).V(AP)
4. AB_Length_Squared = |V(AB)|^2
5. D = Proj / AB_Length_Squared
